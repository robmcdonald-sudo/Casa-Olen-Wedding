Accommodation

Where to Stay

📍 See Casa Olen on the map

In and around Agliano Terme are a number of small guest houses, bed and breakfasts, agriturismo (simple rooms, usually on a farm), apartments and Airbnbs. Most of the ones we list are close to us, between 10–20 minutes' walk.

Accommodation in Agliano that is walking distance to us is very limited. We have suggested the closest ones. If you can't find something on the lists below or on the web, and don't want to drive yourselves, we would suggest looking in Asti, which has a lot more options. Asti is connected to us by bus during the day; in the evenings we will look to arrange some return shared taxis at the end of the night.

Logistics

Getting there

Agliano Terme is a small village close to Asti, and we are located 10 minutes' walk outside of the village. Public transport options are described below, but your best bet is to rent a car so you can visit all the nearby villages, vineyards and towns.

By air

Airports

The closest airport (1h10) is Turin, though it has limited services. There are daily flights on Ryanair, BA and easyJet from London Gatwick and Stansted. A train runs direct from Turin Airport to Asti if you don't have a car, then it's a taxi or bus to Agliano.

Milan airports are the next option, with many more flights from all London airports — both Linate and Malpensa are about 1h30–1h45 drive from Agliano. There's no public transport from them without going in and out of Milan and changing multiple times, so it's easiest to rent a car.

Use your Avios on BA to Turin or Milan — it's a bank holiday weekend, but if you book when it opens for sale around 3rd/4th September 2026 (355 days in advance) there are guaranteed seats on every plane and you should have a decent choice of times.

By train

Train

Asti has service from most places in Italy. We have a small branch line to Agliano from Asti a few times a day, or a regular bus service to the village from Asti station, or a taxi.
